Act as a professional data analyst

Your task is to create a fully functional, modern, interactive dashboard in new Sheet.

Requirements:
1. Build a clean and professional UI dashboard (like a business intelligence tool).

2. Include KPI cards at the top:
   - Total Sales
   - Total Profit
   - Total Orders
   - Average Order Value

3. Add visualizations:
   - City-wise sales (bar chart)
   - Category-wise performance (bar/pie chart)
   - Sales Rep performance (bar chart)
   - Monthly sales trend (line chart)


4. Add filters/slicers:
   - Month range filter
   - City filter
   - Category filter
   - Sales Rep filter


Make the dashboard visually appealing, professional, and similar to Power BI/Tableau dashboards.
